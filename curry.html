Description

This is a flavourful and spicy curry, perfect for a cozy dinner at home.


  • 1. Large onion, chopped
  • 2. Green pepper, chopped
  • 3. Potatoes, chopped
  • 4. Rajah, chilli, rosemary, southern grill, salt

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot.
  2. Add onions, green pepper and fry.
  3. Add the pieces of chicken and potatoes, add spices and cook for 30 minutes.
